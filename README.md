# Cleo Fashion

**The AI stylist in your pocket.**  
Personalized fashion advice, virtual try-on, and a social style feed—so everyone can dress with confidence.

---

## The Opportunity

Fashion is a **$2T+** global industry. Shoppers waste time and money on clothes that don’t fit or don’t suit them. Cleo combines **AI styling**, **virtual try-on**, and **social discovery** so users choose better outfits, buy with confidence, and share their style—all in one app.

---

## What We Build

**Cleo** is a cross-platform fashion app (iOS, Android, Web) that acts as a personal stylist and style community.

| Capability | Value |
|------------|--------|
| **AI Stylist** | 24/7 outfit advice, shopping suggestions, and styling tips tailored to the user’s wardrobe and preferences. |
| **Virtual Try-On** | See how garments look on the user before buying—with face and identity preserved for a realistic preview. |
| **Style Feed** | Share looks, like, comment, and discover people with similar style. Algorithm surfaces content that matches taste. |
| **Wardrobe & Calendar** | Organize clothes, get outfit suggestions from what they own, and plan outfits by day or event. |
| **Social Layer** | Friend requests, messaging, and notifications so style becomes social and repeat usage grows. |

---

## Why Cleo

- **Full stack** — Styling, try-on, wardrobe, and social in one product; no need to juggle multiple apps.
- **AI-first** — Recommendations and chat are powered by modern AI; experience improves as models improve.
- **Cross-platform** — One codebase for mobile and web; fast iteration and lower cost to scale.
- **Built to scale** — Cloud-native backend, real-time data, and architecture designed for growth.

---

## Technology

Cleo is built for performance and scale:

- **Mobile & Web** — Single codebase (Flutter) for iOS, Android, and web.
- **AI & ML** — Conversational AI for styling and guidance; computer vision for virtual try-on.
- **Backend & Data** — Scalable APIs, real-time sync, and secure storage.
- **Social & Feed** — Real-time feed, engagement, and discovery built for retention.
